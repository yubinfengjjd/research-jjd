# 判断示例

以下研究、数据和文献关系均为虚构。Source A/B 只作教学占位，正式稿须用已核读的真实来源。

## 直接回答问题并保留不利发现

> Recalibration reduced average prediction error in the external hospitals, but it did not resolve the error in the low-quality subgroup. The benefit therefore applies to the overall hospital populations evaluated here and cannot be assumed for every subgroup. This distinction matters when the score is considered for settings with a higher proportion of low-quality examinations.

主张先行，边界服务于实际发现。必要否定可以直接保留；没有每段一个限定词、每节五次以内等预算。

## 原因未知也能构成讨论

> The persistent error in the low-quality subgroup limits the interpretation of the overall improvement. Image quality was not measured with a common scale across hospitals, so the present analysis cannot distinguish a quality effect from differences in case mix. The cause of the subgroup discrepancy remains unknown.

此段给出证据含义和可区分范围，没有编造机制，也不强制每段提出候选原因或验证实验。

## 文献承担论证角色

> Separating calibration from discrimination helps explain why apparently conflicting external evaluations can coexist. Source A assessed ranking performance after transfer, while Source B measured absolute risk error. Our findings address the latter problem: calibration changed despite similar discrimination. The studies therefore support different claims rather than competing estimates of the same endpoint.

只有假想来源实际支持这些事实时才成立。引用用于比较定义与证据角色，不按作者逐篇目录化；没有固定每段篇数。若不知道差异原因，写明未知即可，不补无据解释。

## 描述性定位与因果

逐阶段对比可以支持“差距在最终输出前已经出现”等描述性定位；不能据此声称某组件造成全部差距。non-inferential 只在确实没有推断分析时适用，不能替换检验未显著、事后检验或未预设阈值。

## 具体结论

> The study identifies a calibration problem that was obscured by discrimination alone. Recalibration improved average error within the evaluated populations, with persistent uncertainty in the low-quality subgroup.

结论回答实际空白且保留边界，不靠“需进一步验证”的空泛尾句替代发现，也不把未解决问题包装成整体成功。
