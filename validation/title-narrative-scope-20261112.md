# Coherent title scope validation — 2026-11-12

Author feedback exposed an editorial failure: short method labels could omit major evaluation work, while broader revisions could become punctuation-separated lists. The title guidance now favors a coherent account of the main research task, method and material evaluation scope. It does not impose minimum words, require every analysis family, or reject an adequate short original title.

Model prefixes and colons are optional. A prefixed title may use a colon; a continuous title may omit both. Commas and dashes are a style judgment rather than a programmatic ban. Proper-name and technical hyphens are retained. Journal constraints and explicit author preferences remain applicable. Generic organizational examples are illustrative, not topic-specific templates.

## Validation performed

- Existing title/submission tests: **63 passed**. No executable checks were changed and no new tests merely matching instructional wording were added. The prior full-suite result belongs to its prior revision and is not presented as a fresh full-suite run here.
- Skill-creator validation passed for the submission and pipeline entrypoints.
- Three fresh isolated forward-test rounds used synthetic CT, asthma and histopathology requests. The evaluator received the skill and raw requests without expected answers or previous-round feedback. Parent review inspected actual candidate titles and rationales.
- Round 1 exposed over-compression of explanation and uncertainty measurements into an undefined reliability label. Guidance was refined to preserve distinct measured properties without banning terminology.
- Round 2 kept those distinctions but still produced unnecessary enumerated title bodies. Guidance added an explicit sentence-level reconstruction check and a generic organizational example.
- Final round results and review are recorded privately; the bounded accepted cases cover broad coherent title scope, preserving an adequate single-objective title, and optional model-prefix/colon forms. These cases do not prove universal narrative quality or journal compliance.
- Four changed rule files were backed up and hash-verified across Codex, Claude Code and Agents. Manuscript content, native citation fields and existing title-selection state were not modified during this maintenance task. No private manuscript text or author information is included in this validation note.
